\chapter{Future Work}
\label{ch:future_work}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:fw_intro}
While the current platform delivers a resilient, secure foundation for government task tracking and alert generation, technological advancements and expanding public sector scale offer compelling trajectories for future enhancements and research investigations.

\section{Planned Enhancements}
\label{sec:planned_enhancements}
Immediate technical enhancements scheduled for subsequent iterations include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Offline-First Mobile Architecture:} Incorporating local SQLite storage and Background Sync Service Workers within the React Expo application to allow field officers to inspect tasks and log proofs in areas with zero cellular connectivity, syncing automatically once connectivity is restored.
    \item \textbf{Decentralized Microservices Migration:} Decomposing the monolithic Express server into independent containerized microservices (e.g., Notification Broker, Authentication Service, Task Analytics Engine) orchestrated via Kubernetes.
    \item \textbf{Automated Document OCR \& Verification:} Integrating optical character recognition (OCR) models to automatically parse physical stamp seals and signatures on submitted PDF proofs before forwarding to department heads.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Research Extensions}
\label{sec:research_extensions}
Promising avenues for academic and algorithmic research include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Machine Learning for Turnaround Time Prediction:} Utilizing regression models and historical department logs to forecast completion delays accurately and dynamically adjust deadlines based on officer workload distributions.
    \item \textbf{Automated Task Assignment Optimization:} Formulating integer linear programming (ILP) algorithms to allocate tasks across departmental staff while minimizing overload and maximizing geographical proximity.
\end{itemize}

\section{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethical_considerations}
The deployment of persistent performance monitoring systems in public administration introduces critical ethical imperatives:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Surveillance and Employee Welfare:} Performance telemetry must be utilized to identify systemic bottlenecks and resourcing deficits, rather than punitive surveillance that could induce toxic workplace stress.
    \item \textbf{Data Privacy and Redaction:} Task attachments frequently contain sensitive citizen personally identifiable information (PII). Future builds must incorporate automated PII masking and zero-knowledge encryption protocols.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary}
\label{sec:fw_summary}
The identified enhancements outline a comprehensive roadmap transitioning this platform from a robust operational prototype into an intelligent, adaptive, and ethically grounded public governance framework.
