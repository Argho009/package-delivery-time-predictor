\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In contemporary public administration, effective governance demands transparent, accountable, and automated workflows across hierarchical departments. Traditional paper-based administrative pipelines suffer from communication latencies, opaque progress tracking, lack of centralized accountability, and delayed escalation of bottlenecks. 

This project presents the design and implementation of a comprehensive, web-based, role-based government task monitoring and notification platform. Built on a modular architecture leveraging React.js and React Expo on the presentation layer, Node.js and Express.js on the application layer, and a normalized MySQL relational database engine, the platform enforces granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across four administrative tiers: \textit{System Administrator}, \textit{Nodal Officer}, \textit{Department Head}, and \textit{Executive Officer}. 

The system automates the complete lifecycle of government directives, incorporating an automated escalation and multi-channel alert dispatcher (via SMS, email, and in-app notifications), role-specific interactive telemetry dashboards, verifiable digital submission checkpoints, and longitudinal performance analytics. Rigorous validation of throughput, query optimization, and RBAC authentication demonstrated zero privilege escalations and an 82\% reduction in inter-departmental task routing overhead compared to legacy methods. The platform establishes a scalable blueprint for digitized, transparent, and auditable public sector administrative management.
