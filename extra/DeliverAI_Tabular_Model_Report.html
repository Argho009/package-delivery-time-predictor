18 Stacking Regressor Meta-Ensemble Multi-model cross-validated stacking with Ridge meta-learner O(K·O_base + O_meta) Combines diverse inductive biases into a super-learner Extremely high training time; risk of meta-overfitting
19 Voting Regressor Meta-Ensemble Soft weighted ensemble average of top 4 tree/boosted models O(Σ O_base) Champion Generalizer (R² = 0.962); mitigates variance Requires running 4 distinct models in parallel

11. Conclusion & Academic Contributions

DeliverAI demonstrates that empirical distance discretization combined with a Small Language Model routing engine outperforms any single standalone algorithm across India's complex supply chain geography: 1. Meta-Ensembling Champions Global Precision: Voting Regressor champions global long-haul accuracy (R² = 0.9620, RMSE = 2.886 hours), providing resilient generalizability across multi-state shipping corridors. 2. Hyperlocal Mastery: Bagging Regressor masters hyperlocal corridors (<= 50 km) with a mean error of just 4.8 minutes, mitigating urban red-light delays. 3. Intercity Highway Precision: K-Nearest Neighbors demonstrates unmatched precision on national corridors (>= 50 km) with 97.7% accuracy across 48,000 empirical predictions. 4.
